Frequently Asked Questions & Problem Solving

Common Exam Photo Upload Mistakes & Solutions

Real, verified technical answers to the most common rejection reasons on SSC, UPSC, and IBPS servers.

Why do government exam portals reject photos even when the file size is below 50 KB? +

File size (KB) is only one of three server validation criteria. Exam upload portals typically validate:

  • Exact Pixel Dimensions: For example, SSC requires approximately 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm (around 138 × 177 pixels at standard screen resolution, or 350 × 450 px on newer portals). If dimensions deviate significantly, the automated portal script aborts with an error.
  • MIME / Format Header: Many applicants simply rename a .png file to .jpg. Exam servers inspect the binary magic bytes, detecting a corrupt header. This tool decodes and re-encodes pure JPEG binary streams.
  • Aspect Ratio Distortion: Uploading a landscape selfie squeezed into passport dimensions causes biometric facial rejection.
How does this tool compress photos to exact KB limits without making them blurry? +

Standard compressors perform a single lossy pass at a fixed quality (e.g. 80%), which produces arbitrary file sizes depending on image texture. Our engine utilizes a binary search iterative loop on HTML5 Canvas: it dynamically adjusts quantization matrices until the resulting Blob size falls precisely within 1–3 KB below your selected ceiling. This maintains maximum sharpness while guaranteeing strict portal compliance.

Is it safe to upload my personal photo and signature here? +

Yes, 100% safe. Unlike most photo resizing websites, ExamResizer processes every image locally in your browser using JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas API. Your photograph or signature is never uploaded across the network to our server, never written to a disk, and never stored in any database. You can even disconnect your internet after loading the page, and the tool will continue to resize and download normally.

What are the official photo rules for SSC CGL and CHSL? +

According to the official Staff Selection Commission notifications:

  • Photo Dimensions: 3.5 cm (width) × 4.5 cm (height).
  • Photo File Size: 20 KB to 50 KB (JPEG format).
  • Background: Light / Plain white background. Both ears must be clearly visible. Cap, goggles, and masks are strictly forbidden.
  • Signature Dimensions: 4.0 cm (width) × 2.0 cm (height).
  • Signature File Size: 10 KB to 20 KB (JPEG format).
How do I remove grayish or yellow shadows from my mobile photo of a signature? +

When you sign on white paper and photograph it indoors with a phone, the paper appears dull gray or shadowed, which causes portal rejection. Simply toggle the "Signature Background Clean-up & Contrast Boost" switch in our workspace. It executes a high-pass luminance filter on the canvas pixels, mapping near-white pixels to pure #FFFFFF while darkening the ink strokes to deep crisp black.

What is the difference between NEET Passport and Postcard Photo? +

The National Testing Agency (NTA) for NEET UG requires two distinct photographs: a standard Passport Size photograph (10 KB to 200 KB) for the admit card, and a larger 4" × 6" Postcard Size photograph (10 KB to 200 KB) with white background, applicant name, and date of photograph printed beneath the portrait.
